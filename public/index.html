Meet Pando.

Pando is The Good Span’s assistant. In a relaxed 10–15-minute conversation, Pando explores six areas that contribute to healthy ageing and help reduce the risk of preventable health conditions: nutrition, sleep, movement, mind, social connection and preventive health. Your answers help build a picture of your habits and identify opportunities to support your long-term health. There are no right or wrong answers, and you can skip any question.

At the end, you’ll get

  • A personalised report showing where you are in each of the six areas.
  • Personalised recommendations: habits and routines chosen for you from evidence-based practices.
  • A simple way to fit them into your week.
